Privacy

Your creations.
Your device.

Fotobox stores your designs locally. No developer cloud, advertising or tracking. Apple handles purchases. This policy also explains website hosting and support emails.

Controller and contact

Rob Bertram is responsible for Fotobox and this website. For privacy questions and support, email rob_bertram@icloud.com.

Your content in the app

Dimensions, projects and imported photos are processed and stored locally on your device. The app does not run a developer server for this content and contains no advertising, usage analytics or tracking SDKs. No user account is required.

Photos and PDF files

The native photo picker provides only the images you select. Fotobox creates a reduced-size copy without the original EXIF metadata. PDFs are generated locally. Files are sent to another app, printing service or storage destination only when you choose to print, share or save them. Those recipients have their own privacy policies.

Purchases through Apple

Apple processes in-app purchases, payments and subscriptions. Fotobox checks purchase evidence supplied by StoreKit to unlock Pro features. The app does not receive or store bank or credit card details. Purchasing and restoring may require internet access. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Deletion and retention

Saved projects remain on your device until you delete them or local app data is removed. Uninstalling the app may remove local content. Exported PDFs and copies held by other services must be deleted separately. Uninstalling does not cancel an Apple subscription. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

When you contact support

If you email support, we process your address, message and any attachments you choose to send in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for app use or contractual requests, otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for our legitimate interest in answering enquiries. Information is retained only as long as necessary to handle the request or meet legal retention requirements. Please send only what is necessary.

This website

This static website has no analytics tools, advertising trackers, contact forms or remotely loaded fonts added by us. Requests necessarily send technical connection data such as IP address, time, requested page and browser information to the hosting infrastructure. The site is provided through GitHub Pages. For information about GitHub’s processing, see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Processing serves delivery and security under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The providers process technical data under their own operational and security arrangements; we add no visitor analytics.

Your rights

Where personal data is processed, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability subject to the applicable legal conditions. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your particular situation. You can complain to a data protection authority. The developer has no remote access to content held only on your device; manage that content through the app and your device.

Changes

These notices describe the stated functionality of Fotobox and will be updated when functions or processing change. Last updated: 17 October 2026.
