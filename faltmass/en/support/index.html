Let’s get making.

Practical answers for designing, printing and making your paper boxes.

How do I print at the correct size?

Export the PDF and choose Actual Size or 100% in your print settings. Turn off Fit to Page. Measure the check line afterwards: it must be exactly 50 mm long.

The template does not fit my paper. What should I do?

Choose A3 instead of A4 or reduce the box dimensions. A base and separate lid share a sheet when there is enough room; otherwise they use two sheets. The app does not silently scale the template down.

How do I add photos and colours?

Open the box designer, rotate the box and select a face. Choose a colour or image. You can move, zoom and rotate imported images within the crop.

Where are my saved projects?

Open Projects to edit or duplicate a saved design. Projects are stored locally. Uninstalling the app may remove them, so export important templates first.

What is free and what requires Pro?

Dimensions, design, previews and projects are free. Faltmaß Pro unlocks PDF export. The app shows currently available purchase options and prices before you buy.

My purchase is not recognised. What can I do?

Use the same Apple Account you used to buy and choose Restore Purchases in the app. Purchases and restoration require internet access. Pending or unverified purchases unlock only after Apple confirms them.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

On your device, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions and choose Faltmaß. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. A one-time purchase is not a subscription.

Why is the finished box slightly smaller?

Dimensions are measured between fold lines. Actual internal dimensions depend on paper or card thickness, folds and glue. Test the fit with a simple paper prototype first.
